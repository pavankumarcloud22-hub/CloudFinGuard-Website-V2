BEFORE WE START

Clear expectations from day one.

How is the engagement priced?

Pricing follows discovery and the agreed scope. The proposal defines deliverables, timelines, access requirements and exclusions before work begins.

Are savings guaranteed?

No fixed percentage is promised. Opportunities are validated against workload needs, operating costs and business constraints before implementation.

Will you make changes to production?

Assessments begin with least-privilege, preferably read-only access. Implementation is separately scoped and requires written approval and agreed validation and recovery steps.

Does monthly support include 24/7 incident response?

No. Monthly advisory is a scoped review and improvement service. Any operational support, availability and response expectations must be explicitly agreed.
