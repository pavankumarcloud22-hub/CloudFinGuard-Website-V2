FINOPS · DELIVERY APPROACH

How to approach AWS cost optimization safely

A useful recommendation explains the cost opportunity and the operational consequences of acting on it.

01 / Discover

Establish the scope, owners and billing baseline. Identify the services and usage patterns that deserve closer investigation.

02 / Analyze

Review utilization, pricing commitments, storage patterns and allocation gaps. Separate recurring usage from short-lived anomalies.

03 / Validate

Check dependencies, workload criticality, recovery needs and the business owner’s requirements before treating a resource as waste.

04 / Recommend

For each opportunity, document current cost, the proposed action, estimated impact, risk and required validation. State assumptions clearly.

05 / Approve

Agree on the implementation scope, change window, owner, success criteria and recovery approach. If revisions are needed, refine the recommendation and seek approval again. Implement only after client approval.

06 / Implement

Execute approved changes in stages with appropriate backups, a rollback plan, suitable access controls and an audit trail. Stop and reassess if observed behavior differs from expectations.

07 / Verify

Confirm workload health and actual cost impact against the baseline and agreed acceptance checks. If checks fail, investigate and remediate, roll back when appropriate, then verify again.

08 / Document outcomes

After acceptance checks pass, document the outcome and assign ownership for monitoring savings and recurring waste.

Example: an unattached EBS volume

Confirm its owner, dependencies and recovery requirements. Validate the backup and obtain approval before cleanup. Measure the resulting cost change after implementation.
